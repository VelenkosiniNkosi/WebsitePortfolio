My Process

How I Work

Whether I'm building a web app or analysing a business system, I follow a structured process that ensures real problems get solved — not just delivered.

01

Understand the Problem

I start by asking the right questions. Who are the users? What is the business trying to achieve? I gather requirements from stakeholders before writing a single line of code or a single diagram.

02

Define & Document

I translate business needs into clear documentation — user stories, UML diagrams, process flows, or technical specifications. This step prevents misunderstandings before development begins.

03

Design & Build

For development work, I plan the architecture first — database structure, API design, component breakdown — then build iteratively, testing as I go rather than all at the end.

04

Test & Validate

I test against the original requirements, not just for bugs. For BA/SA work this means validating that the solution actually solves the business problem. For dev work it means functional and user testing.

05

Communicate Findings

I present results in plain language. Technical details belong in reports, not in stakeholder conversations. I've done this in real settings — including a 30-minute Q&A presentation to the Massmart team.

06

Improve Continuously

Every project teaches me something. I document what worked, what didn't, and how to do it better next time — a habit I built across five Demonstrator roles and one industry internship.
