Online Retail BI Dashboard

A complete end-to-end Business Intelligence project built across 7 days from raw CSV dataset to a professional 6-page executive Power BI dashboard.

The Problem

A UK-based online retailer had 541,909 rows of raw transactional data sitting in a flat CSV file with no structure, no analysis, and no way for leadership to make data-driven decisions. The data included mixed cancellations, NULL customer IDs, encoding errors, and no date intelligence.

What I Built

Starting with Python, I ingested the raw data into a SQL Server staging table using pandas and sqlalchemy, handling real-world data quality issues before a single transformation was applied.

I then designed a star schema data warehouse from scratch - DimDate (730 rows), DimCustomer (4,381), DimProduct (3,848), and FactSales (541,799 rows) - with surrogate keys, referential integrity checks, and a full SQL ETL pipeline that validated £10,644,560.42 in total revenue against the source data with zero orphaned foreign keys.

On top of the warehouse I built 5 analytical SQL views in a dedicated reporting schema covering monthly trends, product performance, geographic revenue, return rates, and day of week patterns. These fed directly into a Power BI data model where I wrote 9 DAX measures including time intelligence, ranking, and percentage calculations.

The final deliverable was a 6-page interactive executive dashboard with synced slicers, KPI cards, map visuals, Top N filters, and a Key Insights page with actionable CEO-level recommendations.

Key Findings

  • November peak revenue of £1.5M represented 78% year-on-year growth
  • UK accounts for 75% of all revenue across 38 countries served
  • No Saturday trading confirmed a pure B2B wholesale business model
  • USA identified as highest-risk return market at 38% return rate
  • Average order value of £484 confirmed bulk wholesale purchasing behaviour
  • Overall return rate of 1.71% - healthy benchmark for wholesale retail

UX Research Business Analysis Industry Project Jun–Jul 2025

Makro Digital Experience Audit

A full UX and business analysis audit of Makro's e-commerce platform - web and mobile - delivering prioritised recommendations to senior stakeholders at Massmart, one of South Africa's largest retailers.

The Problem

Makro's digital platform had no formal UX audit on record. Customers were experiencing friction across key touchpoints - from account onboarding through to checkout and post-purchase - but there was no structured analysis to identify where or why drop-offs were happening.

What I Did

I mapped the full customer journey across both the Makro website and mobile app, covering four key flows: onboarding and account creation, product search and filtering, checkout, and post-purchase (order tracking and returns).

Using BA frameworks, I identified over 10 specific friction points - issues that would cause a real customer to abandon the process or lose trust in the platform. Each finding was documented with a clear description of the problem, its likely impact on the customer, and a recommended fix.

The final deliverable was a formal audit report with all findings ranked by priority and business impact. I presented the report in a structured 30-minute session with Q&A to senior stakeholders at Massmart.

Key Findings

  • Onboarding flow had unnecessary steps that increased drop-off risk for new users
  • Search and filtering on mobile was inconsistent with the web experience
  • Checkout process lacked clear progress indicators, reducing customer confidence
  • Post-purchase communication (order status, returns) was unclear and hard to find
  • Mobile UI had spacing and tap-target issues that made key actions harder to complete
  • 10+ friction points identified and ranked by priority across the full journey

Tools & Methods

Customer Journey Mapping UX Heuristic Evaluation Business Analysis Frameworks Stakeholder Presentation Prioritised Recommendations Report Mobile & Web Audit
