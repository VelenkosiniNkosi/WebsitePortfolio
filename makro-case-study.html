Makro Digital Experience Audit

An end-to-end customer journey audit of Makro's website and mobile app — identifying friction points, usability failures, and strategic improvement opportunities for one of South Africa's largest retailers (Massmart, powered by Walmart).

Client Massmart (Makro) — powered by Walmart
Duration 23 June – 18 July 2025 (4 weeks)
My Role UX Researcher & Business Analyst (Team B)

The Problem

Makro's digital platforms — their website and mobile app — are used by millions of South African shoppers. However, the business had not recently conducted a structured, user-centric audit of the full online purchase journey. The question was simple: what does a real customer actually experience from the moment they land on the site to the moment they complete a purchase?

Makro needed an independent, critical-thinking team to walk through the entire journey with fresh eyes, document what was broken or frustrating, and produce actionable recommendations backed by real data — not assumptions.

My Approach

The project was structured as a 4-week consulting engagement with minimal input from the Makro team — we were expected to be proactive, independent, and critical. I applied a combination of UX research methods, business analysis frameworks, and structured documentation to work through the audit systematically.

Week 1

Kickoff & Setup

Aligned on scope, set up accounts, performed initial platform review, and planned the testing approach across web and mobile.

Week 2

Search & Browse Testing

Tested search relevance, navigation, product pages, filters, image quality, and mobile vs desktop responsiveness. Logged and screenshotted all issues.

Week 3

Cart, Checkout & Post-Purchase

Completed a real end-to-end purchase to experience the full transaction flow — including payment, confirmation, and order tracking.

Week 4

Report & Presentation

Compiled all findings into a structured audit report, created recommendations, and delivered a 30-minute presentation with Q&A to the Massmart team.

Scope & Coverage

The audit covered every stage of the online customer journey, across both the Makro website and mobile app. Each area was tested systematically and documented with findings, screenshots, and recommendations.

Journey Stage What Was Tested
User Onboarding Account registration, email verification, login flow, password reset, mobile app installation
Search & Navigation Search relevance and speed, filters and sorting options, category browsing, product discovery
Product Pages Information completeness (descriptions, pricing, availability), image quality, reviews, mobile responsiveness
Cart & Checkout Adding/removing items, promotions visibility, checkout flow, delivery options, payment methods, transaction errors
Post-Checkout Order confirmation (email/SMS/app), order history tracking, returns and refund flow exploration
Key Findings

We identified over 10 distinct usability and business issues across the platform. Below are representative findings across each category, documented using quantitative observations (clicks, time taken) and qualitative analysis.

  • Onboarding friction: The registration and email verification process had multiple unnecessary steps, with unclear error messages that confused new users and increased drop-off risk.
  • Search inconsistencies: Search results returned irrelevant products and the filtering system was difficult to use on mobile — a critical issue for a platform where most SA shoppers browse on their phones.
  • Product page gaps: Several product listings had missing descriptions, unclear stock availability, and low-quality images — directly impacting purchase confidence.
  • Checkout complexity: The checkout flow had more steps than necessary, promotions were not prominently displayed, and some payment method errors were not handled gracefully.
  • Post-purchase communication: Order confirmation and tracking updates were inconsistent across email, SMS, and the app — creating uncertainty for customers after purchase.
  • Opportunities identified: Simplified onboarding, smarter search with better mobile filters, richer product pages, a streamlined 3-step checkout, and unified post-purchase notifications were among the top improvement opportunities presented.
Deliverables Produced

All deliverables were produced independently with minimal guidance from the Makro team, as required by the project scope. The final presentation included a live Q&A session with the Massmart stakeholder team.

Deliverable Details
Daily / Weekly Logs Tracked all steps, bugs, glitches, and observations with screenshots and screen recordings
Audit Report Structured by journey phase — each section covered Findings, Screenshots, Business Impact, and Recommendations
Stakeholder Presentation 30-minute presentation with Q&A — summarised major insights, top 5 pain points, and improvement opportunities
Evidence Appendix All screenshots and recordings catalogued as supporting evidence for each finding

Note: Specific findings, screenshots, and the full audit report are confidential per the project scope agreement with Massmart. The above represents a summary of the work completed.
